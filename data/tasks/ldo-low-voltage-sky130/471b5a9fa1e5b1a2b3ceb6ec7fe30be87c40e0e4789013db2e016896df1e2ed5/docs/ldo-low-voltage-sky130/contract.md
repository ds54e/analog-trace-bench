# LDO-LOW-VOLTAGE-SKY130

Native qualification is required before registration. The [specification](../../specs/ldo-low-voltage-sky130.json) and [measurement contract](measurement.md) define the task. No circuit or reference measurement is supplied. One frozen free MOS/R/C netlist is evaluated on all conditions. Exploration has no case limit and a 90-minute design deadline.

The low rail supplies 1.0 V from 1.15–1.35 V, retaining a 150 mV minimum input/output difference and only 200–500 pF of external decoupling. DC and recovery bands are 3%; the transient envelope remains 8%.
