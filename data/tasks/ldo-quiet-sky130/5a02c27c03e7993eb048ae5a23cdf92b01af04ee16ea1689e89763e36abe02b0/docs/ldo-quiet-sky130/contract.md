# LDO-QUIET-SKY130

Native qualification is required before registration. The [specification](../../specs/ldo-quiet-sky130.json) and [measurement contract](measurement.md) define the task. No circuit or reference measurement is supplied. One frozen free MOS/R/C netlist is evaluated on all conditions. Exploration has no case limit and a 90-minute design deadline.

The quiet rail supplies 1.2 V using 100 nF–1 uF of external decoupling. Output noise, 40/30 dB PSRR at 1 kHz/1 MHz, and line excursions are scored. The recovery deadline is 20 us within the fixed 1% band.
