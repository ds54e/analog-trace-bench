# OTA-WIDE-SKY130

Native qualification is required before registration. The [specification](../../specs/ota-wide-sky130.json) and [measurement contract](measurement.md) define the task. No circuit or reference measurement is supplied. One frozen free MOS/R/C netlist is evaluated on all conditions. Exploration has no case limit and a 90-minute design deadline.

The wide follower window is 0.25–1.10 V at a 1.35–1.65 V supply. The 10 mV tracking and 45 dB CMRR limits prioritize input/output range; precision and load-drive tasks retain their separate targets.
