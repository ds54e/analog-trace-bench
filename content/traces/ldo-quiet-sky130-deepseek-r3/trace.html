1: # LDO-QUIET-SKY130 measurement contract
2: 
3: Numerical stimuli and limits come from spec.json. Connect the same submitted
4: DUT to VIN, VOUT, VSS and ideal noiseless VREF. The load is a prescribed ideal
5: finite-slew current sink; the external capacitor is ideal and bench supplied.
6: No startup, protection, bandgap, yield or layout checks are implied.
7: 
8: ## DC and load changes
9: 
10: Run DC at all three loads and light/heavy, middle/heavy and burst profiles.
11: Meter all DUT terminals. Input/reference currents enter DUT; output/ground
12: currents leave. Verify KCL within 1 nA. Overhead is
13: max(Iin-Iout,0)+max(Iref,0), including feedback and bias consumption.
14: Compare voltage against the fixed 1.2 V target, never the waveform tail.
15: Both isolated edge directions and the final falling burst edge have recovery
16: windows. Recovery is the final interpolated entry into the absolute required
17: band that remains there until the window ends. Retain overshoot, droop, initial
18: hold error, profile points and all signed currents.
19: 
20: Maximum sample gap is 1e-08 s. Every required window must be fully
21: covered with finite increasing data. Unsettled complete data retains null
22: recovery and an observation lower bound; it is MISS when that bound exceeds the
23: limit. Missing/truncated/invalid data is measurement failure. Unexecuted cases
24: are incomplete, never PASS. Inclusive limits are compared without rounding.
25: 
26: ## Supply measurements
27: 
28: Line stimulus starts at minimum VIN, rises to maximum, and returns to minimum,
29: at the group's highest load, process, temperature and capacitance. The starting
30: VIN label does not add a different line trajectory. PSRR saves native VIN/VOUT
31: phasors at 1 kHz and 1 MHz and reports 20 log10(abs(VIN/VOUT)). Check the native
32: regulated OP; zero/nonfinite transfers cannot produce infinite rejection.
33: 
34: PSRR, bidirectional line excursion/recovery and output noise are scored in every
35: public and independent group. Each nominal group contains 11 native processes:
36: three DC, three load profiles, two PSRR, two noise and one line profile.
37: There are 198 public plus 33 independent cases, with no duplicate nominal
38: characterization stage. Standalone noise_low/noise_high/line/PSRR cost one each.
39: Line uses the tighter line excursion limit; its initial and recovery bands are
40: the fixed DC/recovery bands. Repeated endpoint trajectories across starting-VIN
41: labels are repetitions, not extra coverage.
42: 
43: Output noise integrates native output voltage ASD squared from 10 Hz to 100 kHz
44: at 50 points/decade. Use a linear-frequency trapezoidal PSD integral and then its
45: square root. VREF and load are ideal noiseless sources. Keep the spectrum in
46: V/sqrt(Hz), integrated output in V RMS, and a valid regulated OP. Negative ASD,
47: missing band endpoints, sparse/nonfinite data and nonregulating OP cannot pass.
48: 
49: ## Numerical precision

52: Numerical precision comparisons use a separately authorized developer allocation.
53: A standard-grid PASS is not a per-submission convergence proof.

Topology

  • Source-degenerated PMOS mirror load (20 kΩ each, 30 pF bypass) — dominates the 1/f noise budget in sky130, cutting output noise ~7× to 20–23 µV RMS.
