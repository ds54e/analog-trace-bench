* ATB OTA-SKY130 DUT -- two-stage Miller-compensated OTA on SKY130 1.8 V devices
*
* Topology: M1/M2 are the NMOS differential pair with common source ntl.
* M3 is the diode-connected PMOS load at na; M4 mirrors it into n1.  M7 is
* the PMOS second stage (source vdd, gate n1, drain vout), loaded by the
* NMOS current sink M8 (drain vout, source vss).  Diode-connected Mbn accepts
* ibias; its gate also biases the NMOS tail device Mtl and M8.
* Miller compensation connects n1 to vout through Cc and optional series Rz.
* For rz = 0, render_dut connects Cc directly to vout and omits Rz.
* The netlist below is authoritative.  docs/figures/ota-sky130.svg and the
* editable project in circuits/ota-sky130/schematic/ are derived documentation,
* not sizing or evaluation sources.
*
* Static polarity (large-signal, checked in bench tb_dc):
*   A rise on vin_p raises the M2 current and lowers the M4 current; node n1
*   falls; the PMOS M7 gate falls, so M7 sources more current and vout rises.
*   Hence vin_p is the NON-inverting input and vin_n (gate of M1, whose drain
*   is the diode-connected node na) is the inverting input of the complete
*   two-stage amplifier.
*
* SKY130 devices are PDK subcircuits, so every device below is an X instance.
* Body terminals are tied to the supply rails; there is no separate bulk port
* in P0.  The DUT contains no behavioural source.
