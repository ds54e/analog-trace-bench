1: # Always-on LDO measurement contract
2: 
3: Numerical stimulus settings come from spec.json. Loads are 1/20/100 uA;
4: external capacitors are 10/100 nF. Edges take 2 us, initial hold is 100 us,
5: isolated plateaus are 200 us, and maximum sample gap is 50 ns. Burst plateaus
6: are 20 us with eight pulses and a 200 us tail. DC error and recovery use the
7: absolute 1.176–1.224 V band; maximum transient excursion is 96 mV from 1.2 V.
8: Recovery must complete within 100 us. Evaluate both load-edge directions.
9: Minimum-load DC overhead is at most 2 uA, other DC loads at most 10 uA.
10: Absolute VREF current is at most 1 uA. Use the signed terminal currents;
11: overhead = max(Iin-Iout,0)+max(Iref,0). Preserve KCL within 1 nA.
12: No startup, protection, noise or yield requirement is included.
13: 
14: Each standalone DC, load profile, line profile or PSRR condition costs one native
15: case. A full nominal group has three DC cases and three load profiles (six cases).
16: Line transient and light/heavy-load PSRR at 1 kHz/1 MHz are reference measurements,
17: not scored. PSRR is 20 log10(abs(VIN/VOUT)) at the regulated operating point.
18: Line stimulus goes from minimum to maximum VIN and back at heavy load.
19: 
20: Save full waveforms and OP currents. Settling is final entry into the fixed
21: recovery band that remains there through the observation end. Check initial
22: hold DC error and maximum post-edge excursion independently. Burst recovery is
23: checked after the final falling edge. A complete waveform that never recovers
24: has a null recovery plus its observation lower bound and fails if that bound
25: exceeds the limit. Truncated or invalid samples are measurement failures.
26: One submitted circuit must pass all required conditions without rounding.
27: 
28: ## Numerical precision
29: 
30: Independent evaluation uses the captured standard grid for every required case.
31: Numerical precision comparisons use a separately authorized developer allocation.
32: A standard-grid PASS is not a per-submission convergence proof.
